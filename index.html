Privacy Policy for PagePress

Last updated: October 2, 2026  ·  Effective date: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

PagePress (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a PDF scanner and converter application.

We built PagePress with privacy as a core principle. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

By using PagePress, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Our Privacy Principles

  • Your documents stay on your device. All scanning, image processing, PDF creation, conversion, and editing happen locally on your device.
  • We do not operate document servers. We do not upload, store, or process your scanned documents or PDF files on any external servers.
  • No account required. You can use the App without creating an account or providing personal information.
  • Minimal data collection. We collect as little information as possible.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

PagePress does not collect, store, transmit, or sell:

  • The content of your scanned documents or PDF files
  • Photos or images you select for conversion
  • Text extracted via OCR
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or other personal identifiers
  • Location data
  • Contacts, calendar, or messages
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking data
  • Browsing history

Your documents never leave your device unless you choose to share or export them using other apps or services.

4. Information We May Process Locally

The App may temporarily process the following only on your device:

  • Camera images (when you scan documents)
  • Photos or files you select from your device storage
  • Generated PDF files and temporary processing files

These are stored only in the App’s local storage or the location you choose. Temporary files created during processing are deleted automatically after the operation completes.

5. Permissions

PagePress may request the following permissions solely to provide core features:

Permission Purpose Data Handling
Camera Scan physical documents Processed locally, never uploaded
Photo Library / Storage Select images to convert to PDF or save results Accessed only when you choose files
Files / Storage Save and open PDF files Local access only

You can revoke any permission at any time in your device settings. Revoking permissions may limit some features.

6. Data Storage

  • All documents and settings remain on your device.
  • No cloud backup or synchronization is performed by the App.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all data stored by the App.
  • We have no access to your files.

7. Third-Party Services

PagePress does not use:

  • Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting services that send personal data
  • Cloud document processing services

The App works completely offline for core scanning and conversion features.

If future versions introduce optional features that require network access (e.g., license verification for a Pro version), those will be clearly disclosed and limited to the minimum necessary data.

8. Children’s Privacy

PagePress is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
